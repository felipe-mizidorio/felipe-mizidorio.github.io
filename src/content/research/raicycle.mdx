## About the research

RAICYCLE (*Tecnologias Avançadas para o Processamento e Reciclagem Inteligentes de Resíduos*) develops advanced technologies for intelligent waste processing and recycling. The work was carried out at the Research Centre in Digitalization and Intelligent Robotics (CeDRI).

My goal was a system for detecting and estimating volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in municipal solid waste. The main tasks were:

1. Collecting and designing VOC detection datasets for training machine learning models
2. Implementing classification and estimation models
3. Testing and validating the models
