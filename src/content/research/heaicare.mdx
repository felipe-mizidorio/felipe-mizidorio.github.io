## About the research

heAICare (*Soluções Baseadas em IA para Otimização do Neurodesenvolvimento Feto-Neonatal*) develops AI-based solutions for monitoring, diagnosis and personalised treatment in fetal-neonatal neurodevelopment. The work is carried out at the Research Centre in Digitalization and Intelligent Robotics (CeDRI).

My goal is a sub-millimetre measurement system for 3D objects. The main tasks are:

1. Studying imaging techniques for precise measurement of 3D objects
2. Implementing 3D measurement models
3. Testing and validating the models
